What it is

Fire Guard brings together a Symfony backend API, a web dashboard for monitoring centers, and a mobile application for field teams. Sensors and devices feed data about heat, moisture, and gas in forest areas. When risk rises, operators see alerts on a map, manage centers and assets, and dispatch tasks to brigades—who then use the mobile app to accept missions, navigate to incidents, and update status in real time.

Operations dashboard

Maps, fires, devices, brigades, and history in one place for dispatch and oversight.

Field mobile app

Brigades sign in securely, view assigned tasks, and report from the location of the event.

Backend & API

A unified API powers authentication, data, and integrations for web and mobile clients.

How it works

From sensor signal to coordinated response in four steps.

1

Detection & sensing

Devices in forest zones report environmental readings. Unusual patterns help surface potential fire risk.

2

Visibility on the dashboard

Your operations center sees incidents, geography, and resources on a map and in structured lists.

3

Dispatch & tasks

Operators assign work to fire brigades and track progress from assignment through completion.

4

Brigade execution

Teams use the mobile app to open tasks, navigate, request help when needed, and update status on site.

Who it is for

Fire Guard is built for operations centers coordinating forest coverage, fire brigades working in the field, and forest and civil protection organizations that need a single operational view across sensors, people, and incidents.
